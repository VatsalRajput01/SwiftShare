About SwiftShare

SwiftShare was born from a simple idea. File sharing should be fast, private, and incredibly easy. We grew tired of services that required sign-ups, tracked user data, or were cluttered with ads and complicated features. We wanted to create a tool that does one thing and does it perfectly: get your file from point A to point B securely.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a seamless and private file transfer experience for everyone. We believe you shouldn't have to trade your privacy for convenience. Whether you're a developer sending code, a designer sharing a mockup, or just a friend sending a photo, SwiftShare is built to be the quickest and safest bridge for your data.

What We Stand For

  • Simplicity : No accounts. No complex dashboards. Just upload, get a code, and download.
  • Privacy : We don't want your data. Files are permanently deleted after expiry or download. We don't track what you send.
  • Security : With end-to-end principles in mind, optional password protection gives you an extra layer of security for sensitive files.
  • Speed : Our infrastructure is optimized for fast uploads and downloads, so you can stop waiting and keep moving.

This project is a passion, and we're constantly working to make it better, more secure, and more reliable. Thank you for trusting us with your files.
